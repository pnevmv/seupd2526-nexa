\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Task 1 of CheckThat! 2026 closely resembles Subtask 4b of CheckThat! 2025, with the primary distinction being the introduction of multilingual datasets.
To contextualize prior methodologies, we consulted the CLEF-2025 CheckThat! Lab Task 4 overview paper \cite{OverviewCheckthat2025task4b}.
In particular, the work by the Deep Retrieval team \cite{deepRetrieval} was of special interest to us due to their successful integration of both dense and sparse retrieval techniques.
